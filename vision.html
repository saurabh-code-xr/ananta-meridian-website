Where it can lead

Wider environments, more sources, shared understanding across fleets. Each step earns its place with evidence first.

Why north

The hard places come first.

GPS is denied or degraded at high latitude, underground and at sea. Cold, distance and disconnection punish systems that cannot judge their own data.

That is where an honest second opinion matters most, and where Canadian engineering has reason to lead.
