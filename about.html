About

A small Canadian company with a narrow focus.

Ananta Meridian Inc. is building an independent trust layer for autonomous systems. We are based in Mississauga, Ontario.

Who we are

Engineers first.

Ananta Meridian Inc. is a Canadian company, registered under the Controlled Goods Program. We are early stage and pre-revenue, and we say so.

We integrate allied sensing, ISR and edge-AI technology, and we develop our own trust layer on top of it. Our focus is the places where sensors fail quietly.

Origin

Toronto is where it started.

The Gardiner at night, the waterfront, the port: infrastructure that works because someone measured it carefully. That discipline is the brand. The work points north.
