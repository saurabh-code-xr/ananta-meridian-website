We're early. That's the honest pitch — a small Toronto team integrating allied autonomous systems and building our own sensor fusion IP from the ground up, for people who want to shape foundational work rather than join it after it's decided.

Where we actually are.

Ananta Meridian is an active integrator with real OEM partnerships, CGP registered, working on a sensor fusion engine that's been validated in simulated degraded sensing scenarios — with hardware integration underway.

We are not a manufacturer yet, and we don't have deployed systems or operational customers to point to. If you want a stable, defined role at a mature company, that's a fair thing to want — it isn't this, yet.

What that means for you

Foundational scopeEarly decisions on fusion architecture, sensing pipelines, and platform integration are still being made.
Direct accessA small team means your work reaches the founder and customers directly, without layers.
Real constraintsCompliance, CGP governance, and Canadian defence procurement realities shape how fast we can move — and why.
