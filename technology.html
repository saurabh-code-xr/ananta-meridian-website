Ananta Meridian Own IP

Confidence-aware sensor fusion.

We are developing a sensor fusion engine purpose-built for Arctic and maritime environments, where individual sensors routinely degrade, drop out, or conflict with one another.

The system fuses heterogeneous inputs — acoustic, RF, AIS, environmental — into a single operator-facing confidence output with recommended actions. It is hardware-agnostic by design, built to integrate with allied edge AI platforms rather than compete with them.

01 Heterogeneous sensor input Acoustic · RF · AIS · ENV
02 Quality & freshness weighting Degraded input handling
03 Reliability memory Per-sensor historical tracking
04 Adversarial detection Spoofing & miscalibration flags
05 Confidence output HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW + operator actions
Validated in simulated degraded sensing scenarios · Hardware integration underway
