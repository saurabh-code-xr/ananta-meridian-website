DOMAINS
Operating Domains

Where the mission
demands more.

Five environments where assumptions fail and infrastructure runs thin. The same capability model — Sense, Understand, Decide, Act — applies across all of them, configured to the operational reality of each.

01 / Air & Counter-UAS

Detection-first awareness for low-altitude threats.

Layered sensing across acoustic, RF, optical, and radar modalities gives defended perimeters, airfields, and critical assets continuous situational awareness. Passive early warning adds no active emission of its own — a meaningful advantage where staying quiet matters.

When a response is warranted, counter-UAS interceptors and persistent ISR provide the follow-through — from initial detection to confirmed track to response.

02 / Maritime & Underwater

Port awareness and offshore monitoring.

AIS fusion, aerial maritime ISR, and subsurface sensing bring persistent monitoring to ports, coastal infrastructure, and offshore energy assets — including in environments where communications are intermittent and coverage gaps are the norm.

This domain is a proving ground for our own sensor fusion IP, purpose-built for maritime and Arctic conditions where individual sensors routinely lose signal.

03 / Arctic & Remote

Persistent awareness where infrastructure ends.

Cold-weather autonomous platforms, satellite-linked sensing, and confidence-aware fusion are designed for environments where individual sensors routinely degrade or fail — and where the nearest technician might be a flight away.

This is the founding use case for our sensor fusion engine: validated in simulated degraded sensing scenarios, with hardware integration underway.

04 / Critical Infrastructure

Site security for high-consequence assets.

Integrated sensing and detection for energy facilities, transport hubs, border crossings, and utility infrastructure — early warning, classification, and cueing without adding active RF footprint to the site.

Built for practical, risk-aware operators: capability that fits existing security workflows rather than replacing them.

05 / Space-Enabled Awareness

Orbital connectivity for terrestrial missions.

LEO satellite links connect remote sensors, Arctic platforms, and maritime assets to command and control — resilient communications where terrestrial networks are unavailable or unreliable.

Space-enabled connectivity is what makes the other four domains persistent rather than intermittent, closing the gap between remote sensing and a usable operator decision.
