Capability Model

Sense. Understand. Decide. Act.

Effective autonomy begins long before a platform moves. It begins with trusted sensing, resilient data, and clear human decisions.

01
Sense

Detect signals across acoustic, RF, optical, infrared, radar, sonar, and satellite spectrums — in degraded, contested, and environmentally stressed conditions.

  • Passive acoustic early warning
  • RF spectrum monitoring
  • Multi-spectral EO/IR
  • AIS maritime tracking
  • Space-enabled awareness
02
Understand

Process, classify, and fuse heterogeneous sensor data at the edge. Produce reliable situational understanding even when individual inputs are degraded or conflicting.

  • Confidence-aware sensor fusion
  • Edge AI classification
  • Reliability-weighted processing
  • Degraded environment operation
  • Digital twin integration
03
Decide

Surface clear, confidence-weighted outputs to human operators. Reduce ambiguity, improve decision timing, and route alerts into the right workflow.

  • HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW confidence
  • Recommended operator actions
  • C2 cueing and handoff
  • Alert routing and escalation
  • Watch-floor integration
04
Act

Deploy autonomous platforms — air, surface, and ground — configured for the mission. ISR, monitoring, inspection, and detection across complex environments.

  • Micro & tactical ISR UAVs
  • Persistent fixed-wing platforms
  • Counter-UAS interceptors
  • Jam-proof tethered ISR
  • Heavy-lift payload platforms

Intelligence that holds when the environment doesn't.
