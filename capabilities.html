SENSE
Capability Model

Sense. Understand.
Decide. Act.

Effective autonomy begins long before a platform moves. It begins with trusted sensing, resilient data, and clear human decisions. This is the framework behind every system we integrate.

01 / SENSE

Detect signals others miss.

Modern environments speak constantly — acoustic, RF, optical, infrared, radar, sonar, satellite. The mission starts with capturing those signals reliably, including in degraded, contested, and environmentally stressed conditions where active systems can't be relied on alone.

We integrate layered sensing so no single input is a point of failure — passive detection that adds no active emission, paired with optical and RF coverage for a fuller picture.

Passive Acoustic Early Warning RF Spectrum Monitoring Multi-Spectral EO/IR AIS Maritime Tracking Space-Enabled Awareness
02 / UNDERSTAND

Turn signals into situational understanding.

Raw sensor data is not understanding. We process, classify, and fuse heterogeneous inputs at the edge — producing reliable situational understanding even when individual sensors are degraded, stale, or conflicting.

This is where our own sensor fusion IP does its work: weighting inputs by quality and freshness, tracking per-sensor reliability over time, and flagging inputs that look spoofed or miscalibrated.

Confidence-Aware Sensor Fusion Edge AI Classification Reliability-Weighted Processing Degraded Environment Operation Digital Twin Integration
03 / DECIDE

Give operators clarity, not noise.

Understanding only matters if it reaches the person who has to act on it. We surface clear, confidence-weighted outputs — HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW — with recommended operator actions, so decisions get faster and more consistent, not slower.

Outputs route into existing command and control workflows, so alerting and escalation fit the way operations centres already work.

HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW Confidence Recommended Operator Actions C2 Cueing & Handoff Alert Routing & Escalation Watch-Floor Integration
04 / ACT

Deploy platforms configured for the mission.

Once sensing, understanding, and decisions are trustworthy, autonomous platforms can act on them — air, surface, and ground systems configured for ISR, monitoring, inspection, and detection across complex environments.

Platform selection is driven by mission need, not catalogue — micro ISR through to persistent fixed-wing and counter-UAS response, sourced from allied OEM partners and configured for the operating environment.

Micro & Tactical ISR UAVs Persistent Fixed-Wing Platforms Counter-UAS Interceptors Jam-Proof Fiber-Optic Tethered ISR Heavy-Lift Payload Platforms
