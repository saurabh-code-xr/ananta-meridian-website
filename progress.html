Progress

Evidence before claims.

What we have done, what we plan, and what we have not yet done. Planned steps are labelled planned.

The evidence ladder

Six steps from desk to Arctic

STEP 00
Prototype testing
Done

Research prototype. Tested in simulation and on recorded and open data.

STEP 01
Parking-garage tests
Planned · next

Our own data, collected during real GPS loss. Listen-only.

STEP 02
Underground mine test
Planned

Listen-only, with an operator who agrees to host it.

STEP 03
Winter test
Planned

Cold-weather behaviour of sensors and of the trust output.

STEP 04
Ocean and maritime test
Target

Moving, wet, reflective environments with a different sensor mix.

STEP 05
Arctic test
Target

The environment that motivates the work, approached last.

STEP 06
Defence experimentation
Target

Evaluation with government test and evaluation partners.

No dates are given. Each step begins when the previous one has produced results we are willing to show.

How we work

Every result is labelled.

Each result is marked measured, simulated or unverified.
We test on data held back from tuning.
We record failures as well as successes, and fix what real data shows us.
